“0 calls observed” means inventory was collected and contained no calls. “Inventory pending” means no inventory is recorded. “Audio Not Available” marks earlier Law 2 dates whose missing audio is no longer retained by OpenMHz; recordings already saved to R2 remain available. Totals describe collected records, not complete radio reception. “Complete” means all inventoried files are saved to R2. Downloaded includes validated files awaiting an R2 checkpoint; Remaining excludes separately listed failures. Main and staging reflect their respective committed archive records, even though both use the same R2 bucket. Refreshing reads the latest published snapshot; it does not start downloads.
