Civil Lawsuit

Michael Anthony Roy filed a federal civil-rights lawsuit in June 2022 alleging that officers arrested him and used excessive force even after realizing he was not the person they were looking for. The parties settled for $93,000 without an admission of liability, and the case was dismissed with prejudice on December 8, 2022.

  • PACER court docket: The filing history for the federal lawsuit, from its June 24, 2022 filing through the dismissal with prejudice entered on December 8, 2022.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Lawsuit — PACER Court Docket

  • Federal complaint: Roy alleges unlawful seizure, excessive force, failure to intervene, and related federal and state claims arising from his mistaken-identity arrest. These are allegations, not court findings.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Lawsuit — Federal Complaint — Document 2

  • Internal affairs report: The investigative report reviewing the arrest, the officers’ actions, and how information about Roy’s identity was communicated. It was filed as an exhibit to the complaint.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Lawsuit — Exhibit 1: Internal Affairs Investigative Report — Document 2-1

  • Injury photographs: Photographs submitted with the complaint to document Roy’s alleged injuries.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Lawsuit — Exhibit 2: Injury Photographs — Document 2-2

  • Civil cover sheet: The basic filing information submitted when the federal lawsuit was opened.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Lawsuit — Civil Cover Sheet — Document 2-3

  • Settlement agreement: The agreement required the defendant parties to pay Roy a combined $93,000 in exchange for releasing his claims and dismissing the lawsuit. It states that no party admitted liability.

  • Michael Anthony Roy — Settlement Agreement With Utah County, American Fork City, and Spanish Fork City

  • Spanish Fork City Council agenda: The November 15, 2022 agenda listed the Roy settlement agreement as a consent item recommended for approval.

  • Michael Anthony Roy Settlement — Spanish Fork City Council Agenda — November 15, 2022
