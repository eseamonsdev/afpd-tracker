Lawsuit

Nancy Boldt and Canary Garden filed a federal lawsuit in November 2020 against American Fork City, the American Fork Police Department, and Officer Jay M. Christensen. The parties later agreed to dismiss the case with prejudice, and the court closed it on April 24, 2023.

  • PACER court docket: The filing history for the lawsuit, from its November 19, 2020 filing through the dismissal with prejudice entered on April 24, 2023.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — PACER Court Docket

  • Federal complaint: Boldt and Canary Garden allege malicious and retaliatory prosecution and other constitutional violations after she was cited for operating without a business license. These are allegations, not court findings.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — Federal Complaint — Document 2

  • Order on the motion to dismiss: The court dismissed some claims while allowing others to continue. The order also dismissed the American Fork Police Department as a separate defendant.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — Order and Memorandum Decision on Defendants’ Motion to Dismiss — Document 27

  • Stipulated motion for dismissal: The parties jointly asked the court to dismiss the case with prejudice.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — Stipulated Motion for Dismissal With Prejudice — Document 48

  • Final dismissal order: The court granted the stipulated motion, dismissed the case with prejudice, and closed it on April 24, 2023.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — Order Granting Dismissal With Prejudice — Document 49

  • Civil cover sheet: The basic filing information submitted when the federal lawsuit was opened.

  • Nancy Boldt Lawsuit — Civil Cover Sheet — Document 1
