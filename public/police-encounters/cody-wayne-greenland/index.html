POLICE ENCOUNTER RECORDS

Cody Greenland

Overview

On April 17, 2020, American Fork police arrested Cody Greenland near the Target parking lot after responding to a report of a man possibly entering vehicles. The linked news coverage and videos show Greenland kneeling with his hands raised before an officer tackles him from behind. Officers then restrain him and use a Taser.

Greenland later filed a federal civil-rights lawsuit alleging unlawful seizure, excessive force, failure to intervene, and failures in department policies, training, and supervision. All five charges in the related criminal case were dismissed with prejudice on August 5, 2021. The civil lawsuit was settled for an undisclosed amount and dismissed with prejudice on July 21, 2022.

Criminal Cases

Utah criminal case 201401103 arose from the April 17, 2020 arrest. Greenland was charged with three counts of possession or use of a controlled substance, one count of use or possession of drug paraphernalia, and one count of interference with an arresting officer. Two possession counts were third-degree felonies; the remaining charges were class B misdemeanors.

All five charges were dismissed with prejudice on August 5, 2021. The detailed case history states that the charges were dismissed pending sentencing on other matters. It records scheduled suppression hearings but does not show a ruling granting a motion to suppress.

Civil Lawsuit

Greenland filed federal case 2:22-cv-00137-RJS on February 28, 2022, against D. T. Cannon, C. Kinnaman, S. Blackburn, Q. Adamson, and American Fork City through its police department. His complaint alleges that officers used excessive force and violated his constitutional rights during the arrest. The attorney planning report states that the defendants denied the allegations and asserted defenses including qualified immunity.

The defendants filed a $25,000 Rule 68 offer of judgment on April 1, 2022. The parties later stipulated to dismissal of all claims with prejudice, and the court entered the dismissal on July 21, 2022. The collected records do not establish that Greenland accepted the April offer or disclose a final settlement amount.
