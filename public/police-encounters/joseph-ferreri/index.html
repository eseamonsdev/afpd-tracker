POLICE ENCOUNTER RECORDS

Joseph Ferreri

Overview

Joseph Ferreri drove his wife to and from the massage parlors where she worked. He was arrested on February 4, 2021. In the probable-cause affidavit, Officer Shawn Lott cited those rides as enabling criminal activity and wrote that Ferreri said he suspected his wife was providing sexual services because she had previously been arrested for similar conduct.

All four charges were later dismissed. According to Ferreri’s federal complaint, the arrest and public accusations deeply affected his life: he lost his job with the Utah Department of Corrections, was turned down three times when he reapplied after the charges were dropped, and was ostracized in his community and taunted by coworkers.

Lawsuit

Ferreri sued American Fork City and Officer Shawn Lott in August 2023. He said Lott relied on racial stereotypes and misleading claims to arrest him, ignored his request for a lawyer, and caused lasting harm to his work and personal life.

The parties reached a settlement on March 17, 2025, and the case was closed on May 13, 2025. The records below do not give the settlement amount.
