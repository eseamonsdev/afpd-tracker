Lawsuit

Janene Thorpe and members of her family filed a federal civil-rights lawsuit in April 2023 against American Fork City, Cedar Hills City, several AFPD officers, and others. The lawsuit was ongoing as of September 2026.

  • PACER court docket: The filing history for the federal lawsuit through September 28, 2026. The latest entries show that motions involving discovery, expert testimony, and summary judgment were still being addressed.

  • Janene Thorpe Lawsuit — PACER Court Docket

  • Federal complaint: The Thorpe plaintiffs allege federal civil-rights violations and related state-law claims involving American Fork City, Cedar Hills City, AFPD, and several officers. These are allegations, not court findings.

  • Janene Thorpe Lawsuit — Federal Complaint — Document 2

  • Civil cover sheet: The basic filing information submitted when the federal lawsuit was opened.

  • Janene Thorpe Lawsuit — Civil Cover Sheet

  • Exhibit A — Parcel map: A parcel map filed with the complaint to show the properties involved in the underlying dispute.

  • Janene Thorpe Lawsuit — Exhibit A: Parcel Map — Document 2-1

Civil Background

Records from three separate Utah civil proceedings involving Janene Thorpe and the Sanches family. These provide background on the underlying neighbor disputes and are separate from the federal civil-rights lawsuit.

  • Civil Stalking Injunction — Case 210400815

    • Maria Sanches obtained a civil stalking injunction against Janene Thorpe in 2021. These records include the temporary and final injunction orders and case histories documenting the proceedings and appeal.
