    KSL’s Andrew Adams interviews Chief Cameron Paul

    KSL confirmed that reporter Andrew Adams interviewed American Fork Police Chief Cameron Paul. KSL described the interview as being about domestic violence and said it would be released at a future date.

  • Clearly Established · October 21, 2026

    Chief Walked Back the LEGO Raid — Now the Transcript Is Out

    Clearly Established attorney Brandon Grable reviews Daniel Sroka’s released transcript of his September 16, 2026 meeting with Chief Cameron Paul. The video includes interview excerpts with Sroka, plaintiffs Steven Allen and Sheldon Norcross, and their attorney, Solomon Radner.

    Grable examines the warrant affidavit, handcuffing and transporting the occupants, Sheldon Norcross’s phone seizure, missing and muted body-camera recordings, redactions, and the chief’s promised follow-up. He also addresses the portions of the police response that the chief continued to defend.

    The video distinguishes the citizen-prepared meeting transcript from an audio recording and attributes the chief’s remarks to that transcript.

  • AFPD Accountability Project · October 17, 2026

    Daniel Sroka’s September 16, 2026 interview with Chief Paul — revised transcript

    Daniel Sroka finished refining the transcript of his September 16, 2026 interview with American Fork Police Chief Cameron Paul.

    The rough transcript was generated using an automated transcription app, then corrected and revised with AI assistance. Eric Seamons and Daniel Sroka subsequently made manual revisions.

  • Clearly Established · October 17, 2026

    Daniel Sroka discusses meetings with Chief Paul on Clearly Established

    American Fork resident Daniel Sroka appeared on Clearly Established to discuss his two meetings with Chief Cameron Paul about the March police encounters involving Reckless Ben and others.

    Sroka described the chief’s changed assessment of the arrests and search warrant, his acknowledgment of mistakes, and the need for public accountability.

    The video also features interviews with plaintiffs Sheldon Norcross and Steven Allen and their attorney, Solomon Radner.
