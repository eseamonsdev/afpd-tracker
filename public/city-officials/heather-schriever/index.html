CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Schriever, Heather

Overview

Heather Schriever serves as American Fork City Attorney. This page collects appointment records and available council remarks relevant to city legal oversight.

Employment

The City Council unanimously approved Schriever’s appointment as City Attorney on January 14, 2025, and approved a legal-services contract with the Schriever Law Firm at the same meeting. June 2026 minutes list her as the city’s legal counsel.

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session Minutes — June 23, 2026

City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.

  • Mayor Frost turned the time over to Heather Schriever. She stated at this time the council should take into consideration evidence submitted that is part of the record and determine whether or not they feel a sufficient amount evidence has been presented to revoke the business license of Millet Massage based on violations of Chapter 5 that have been outlined by Chief Paul. If the council has any specific questions about the ordinances or standards, she would be happy to address them. Ms. Schriever stated if the council wants to consider additional evidence, they can table this. If they feel that substantial evidence has been presented, they could move forward with a decision.
