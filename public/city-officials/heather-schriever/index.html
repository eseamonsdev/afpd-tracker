CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Schriever, Heather J.

Overview

Heather Schriever serves as American Fork City Attorney. This page collects appointment records and available council remarks relevant to city legal oversight.

Employment

The City Council unanimously approved Schriever’s appointment as City Attorney on January 14, 2025, and approved a legal-services contract with the Schriever Law Firm at the same meeting. June 2026 minutes list her as the city’s legal counsel.
