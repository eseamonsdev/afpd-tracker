CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Egner, Cherylyn M.

Overview

Cherylyn Egner previously served as American Fork City Attorney and city legal counsel. This page collects records documenting her service and the later appointment of her successor.

Employment

January 2019 minutes list Egner as legal counsel. The City Council unanimously approved her appointment as City Attorney on January 11, 2022. On January 14, 2025, the mayor referred to thanking Egner for her service, and the council appointed Heather Schriever as City Attorney. The 2022 appointment does not mark the beginning of Egner’s earlier legal work for the city.
