CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Merrill, Tim

Overview

Timothy “Tim” Merrill previously served as American Fork City Attorney. This page collects records documenting his appointment and service as city legal counsel.

Employment

The City Council unanimously approved Tim Merrill’s appointment as City Attorney on April 24, 2018. April 30, 2019 work-session minutes list Timothy Merrill as legal counsel. These records do not establish his exact departure date.

The compensation table documents Tim Merrill’s State of Utah employment as an assistant attorney general from 2020–2025, reported under the name Timothy Merrill. These amounts are state compensation, not payments for his earlier work as American Fork City Attorney.
