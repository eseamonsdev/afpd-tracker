  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 28, 2026:

  • Council Member John honored Brynn Carnesecca, recalling her as the first Miss American Fork with whom he worked as a council member and praising her service as a positive representative of the city. He thanked City staff, Public Works, public safety personnel, Mr. Bunker, the Chamber of Commerce, and Council Member Hunter for the extensive time and effort devoted to Steel Days.
