CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Maurer, Kyle

Overview

Kyle Maurer previously served as American Fork City’s Finance Director and Finance Officer. This page collects records about his role and council remarks concerning police spending.

Employment

January 2018 minutes identify Maurer as Finance Officer in the attendance list and Finance Director in the discussion. January 2019 minutes also list him as Finance Officer. By June 2020, Anna Montoya is listed in that role; these records do not establish Maurer’s exact departure date.

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session Minutes — January 23, 2018
  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session Minutes — January 22, 2019
  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session Minutes — June 9, 2020

City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.
