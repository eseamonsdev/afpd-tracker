  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — September 28, 2021:

  • City Administrator, David Bunker, gave congratulations to Sergeant Ford and Captain Casper for their receiving awards from the VFW. He also mentioned that Planning Commission Chairman Woffinden was excused due to a death in his family. He also reported on the Mental Health Town Hall meeting recently held in which Dr. Christy King spoke about mental health challenges in their community. Also the young lady who serves as Miss American Fork spoke at that meeting, and he stated she did an excellent job. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 9, 2020:

  • Mr. Bunker said that the City’s Public Safety employees were grateful to the Council for the contributions. The City contributes more than is legally required. He clarified that the remaining .27% would be required by the employee, but American Fork continues to be generous to their Public Safety employees. Council Member Barnes commented on the budget impact of $7,000 annually as outlined in the packet. He noted that as they move forward, and more people transition from being tier one to tier two he that the budget impact would continue to rise. Mr. Bunker said that Council Member Barnes was correct. However, as more people became tier two, less people were on tier one which was a higher contribution and cost the City more. Because of this, the transition to tier two was actually beneficial to the City. View minutes, page 8

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — May 14, 2019:

  • Mr. Bunker commented he was pleased with the High School’s accomplishments. He said he was also proud of the Recreation Department. He reported this week was police week and there was an open house at the Police Department on the 15th from 5-7:30. He said they had an outstanding department. He reported the Memorial Day Breakfast would be hosted by the Fire Department. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 22, 2019:

  • Council Member Shelton asked if there was a better way to discuss the inflation in five years. He wanted to know what would happen if there was no consensus. Ms. Egner responded if there was no consensus the rate would stay at 2%. Council Member Shelton asked when the 2% began and Mr. Bunker responded there would be a 2% increase annually. Council Member Shelton said the language was not clear, to which Ms. Egner responded there was no reason to make changes but they could make adjustments. Council Member Shelton asked if there was any way for a party to get out of the agreement. Ms. Egner explained that the parties were required to give 180-day notice but that it would automatically renew every year. Council Member Shelton thought they had wanted a ten-year contract; he did not want to see them pull out after a few years and leave the city to pay for the new station and equipment on our own. Mr. Bunker stated it was automatically renewed for 10 years. Mayor Frost stated that the city needed a second station regardless of what happened with Cedar Hills. He said it would be great to have Cedar Hills participate but they needed to have an opportunity to leave if they were unhappy. This deal was fair for both parties. Council Member Christiansen asked if it was the same thing if they had a ten-year term but still included the 180-day notice. Mr. Bunker responded it would be the same. Council Member Shelton suggested they make the contract be for ten years because they were spending so much money. Mr. Bunker responded it was a one-year contract, but the ten-year portion of the contract did not require Cedar Hills to go out to bid. Council Member Shelton responded they needed to re-work the language to protect the City from another City leaving the contract. Council Member Taylor asked how likely it would be that Cedar Hills left the contract. Mr. Bunker responded that this was different, as Cedar Hills was a member of the Lone Peak Public Safety Board. This was just a service contract and they were not a part of a board. Cedar Hills was hiring the City to provide service. Council Member Carroll asked how the police service was structured. Mr. Bunker responded it was a two-year contract with a 3% increase at the end of the two years. It also had the 180-day clause. Council Member Barnes asked when the 2% increase began, to which Mr. Bunker stated 12 months from now. Council Member Barnes said he had thought the increase began after the fifth year. Mr. Bunker explained how the increase functioned. View minutes, page 3 · View minutes, page 4

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — December 11, 2018:

  • Mr. Bunker reported the Fitness Center was sponsoring a "maintain, don't gain" program. Many in the community and members of staff were participating. He reported they would hold the annual Health and Fitness Fair where blood sugar and heart rate testing would be offered. He also reported the Police Department had an event called Shop with a Cop. Officers took children to breakfast and used a $175 gift card to purchase whatever the children wanted. Mr. Bunker reported they would be holding a Community Survey online for residents to gauge how American Fork City was performing. He encouraged residents to take the survey. He noted the cemetery had a new directory for every gravesite that was covered, lit, and listed everyone who is interred in cemetery. View minutes, page 1 · View minutes, page 2
