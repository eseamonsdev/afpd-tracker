City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.

  • American Fork City Council — Truth in Taxation Public Hearing — August 11, 2026:

  • Mr. Bunker explained that the proposal was for an 8.7% tax increase and that information presented by residents and business owners would help the Council better understand the issue. He said cities may consider tax increases because of inflation, growth, competition for employees, contractors and materials, or increased services. Mr. Bunker stated that public safety was critical for residents and explained that the budget included nine new firefighters for a third fire station. He said the station would cost about $1.5 million annually, with $660,000 coming from the proposed property tax and the remainder from the existing budget. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 28, 2026:

  • Mr. Bunker thanked everyone who attended, volunteered, and contributed to the success of Steel Days. He recognized the many committees and volunteers involved and specifically thanked City staff, law enforcement, Fire and EMS, Public Works, the Parks Department, and the Chamber of Commerce for the significant time and effort devoted to the event. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 23, 2026:

  • Mr. Bunker explained that revenue associated with the proposed property-tax increase would be placed in a separate fund and could not be spent until after the Truth in Taxation hearing and adoption of the final budget. He said the interim budget preserved the Council's flexibility and that the final budget could be revised after the hearing to reflect its priorities, including reallocating funds to support firefighter positions. View minutes, page 9

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — September 28, 2021:

  • Mr. Bunker congratulated Police Sergeant Stuart Fore and Fire Captain Adam Casper for receiving awards from the Veterans of Foreign Wars. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 9, 2020:

  • Mr. Bunker said the City's public-safety employees were grateful to the Council for the retirement contributions. He explained that the City contributed more than legally required and discussed the effect of employees transitioning between retirement tiers. View minutes, page 8

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — May 14, 2019:

  • Mr. Bunker reported that it was Police Week, announced an open house at the Police Department, and said American Fork had an outstanding department. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 22, 2019:

  • Mr. Bunker explained that the agreement would renew automatically and discussed how the contract's term and annual increase would operate. He also stated that the police-services portion was structured as a two-year contract with a 3% increase at the end of two years and a 180-day termination clause. View minutes, pages 3–4

  • Mr. Bunker thanked Officer Garcia, the Police Department, and the Fire Department for their service. He shared a story of officers acting exceptionally and saving lives. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — December 11, 2018:

  • Mr. Bunker reported that the Police Department held its Shop with a Cop event, where officers took children to breakfast and provided each child with a $175 gift card to purchase items they wanted. View minutes, page 1
