Bird, Camden R.

Overview

Camden Bird is American Fork City’s Assistant City Administrator. This page collects records documenting his city administration roles.

Employment

The city’s administration page lists Bird as Assistant City Administrator. April 2019 council work-session minutes identify his earlier role as Administrative Analyst. These records do not establish his exact hire date or the date he became assistant city administrator.
