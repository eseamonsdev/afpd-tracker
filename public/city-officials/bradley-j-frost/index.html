City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — August 25, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost stated that the budget process, which began in February, would conclude that evening. He emphasized that the budget was one of the Council's most important responsibilities because it established priorities and funded services affecting residents. View minutes, page 7

  • American Fork City Council — Truth in Taxation Public Hearing — August 11, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost welcomed everyone to the public hearing. He explained the city was not adopting the certified tax rate that evening; the hearing was to allow the public to comment on the proposed property tax. The City Council would adopt the certified tax rate on August 25, 2026. Mayor Frost later asked citizens to be respectful and courteous, limited comments to two minutes per person, and asked that comments stay on the proposed property-tax rate. View minutes, pages 1–2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 28, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost thanked Josh Walker, the Chamber of Commerce, Mr. Bunker, City staff, and the Police Department for their extensive work, including accommodating approximately 18,000 people at Art Dye Park during the carnival, Big Show, and fireworks. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 14, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost responded that the Council does not engage in dialogue during the public comment portion of the meeting but offered to meet with Mr. Kleiner privately to discuss his concerns in greater detail. He asked Mr. Kleiner to complete a public comment form with his contact information so a meeting could be arranged and stated that he would personally follow up with him. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 9, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost expressed appreciation for the large number of residents who attended and participated in the meeting, thanking them for sharing their perspectives in a respectful and constructive manner. Mayor Frost assured those in attendance that the Council had heard their comments and would carefully consider the issues raised. He encouraged residents to continue participating in future meetings and reaffirmed the Council's commitment to working together with the community to address concerns and find solutions. View minutes, page 5

  • American Fork City Council — Work Session — June 2, 2026:

  • Mayor Frost noted that victims respond differently to law enforcement actions, with some feeling relieved and others struggling because of their relationship with the offender. View minutes, page 13

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — September 28, 2021:

  • Mayor Frost acknowledged the team effort required to protect and serve the City and said the community and its first responders were all in it together. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 12, 2021:

  • Mayor Frost highlighted the Police Department's Shop with a Cop event as one of the positive things done in the community during the week before Christmas. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — May 14, 2019:

  • Mayor Frost said he was pleased about the swearing in of four new American Fork police officers. He paid tribute to Chief Falslev and stated that three of the four recruits were attracted to American Fork because they wanted to work with him. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 22, 2019:

  • Mayor Frost stated that American Fork needed a second station regardless of what happened with Cedar Hills. He said it would be beneficial for Cedar Hills to participate, but the city needed an opportunity to leave if it was unhappy, and he described the agreement as fair to both parties. He also said American Fork had a good working relationship with Cedar Hills. View minutes, pages 3–4

  • Mayor Frost gave examples of Officer Diego Garcia's work in the community and thanked him and the Police Department for their service after Garcia received the NAACP Outstanding Community Service Award. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 23, 2018:

  • Mayor Frost reported that the Police Department handled almost 30,000 service calls, made 233 felony arrests, and averaged 1,252 open investigations during the prior year. He highlighted the department's response to domestic violence, including assigning an investigator full time and providing public education; training officers to administer Narcan; joint police and fire training for active-shooter incidents; and Chief Falslev's participation in legislation and a state policymaking board. He said these actions showed that the community was in good hands with police and fire departments that served proactively rather than reactively. View minutes, pages 13–14
