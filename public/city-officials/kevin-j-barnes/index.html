City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — September 28, 2021:

  • Council Member Barnes re-iterated praise for the people of the police and fire departments. He stated that American Fork, as a community, was truly blessed to have had such fine people serving them. He was also grateful for the work of the VFW in the community. All the selfless service rendered often went unnoticed. He mentioned the death in the family of Planning Commission Chairman Woffinden, stating that the deceased was his grandson [minor’s age and name redacted], who had been very sick for many months. He invited all to keep these people in their thoughts and prayers. In praise of his friend “Woff” he stated that when the Barnes family had moved to American Fork in 1979, the Woffindens lived down the street, and were the first to welcome them into the community, and they had remained friends all those years. They had rendered much service to American Fork. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 9, 2020:

  • Council Member Barnes commented on the budget impact of $7,000 annually as outlined in the packet. He noted that as they move forward, and more people transition from being tier one to tier two he that the budget impact would continue to rise. Mr. Bunker said that Council Member Barnes was correct. However, as more people became tier two, less people were on tier one which was a higher contribution and cost the City more. Because of this, the transition to tier two was actually beneficial to the City. View minutes, page 8

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — May 14, 2019:

  • Council Member Barnes commented he wanted to support personal property rights. He noted this issue affected people’s property rights and neighbors. He said they needed to investigate how this would affect the residents and the police force. He noted he had been called by residents that had issues. He said the police needed to be contacted when there were problems. He said they needed to create an ordinance to get experience. View minutes, page 6

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 22, 2019:

  • Council Member Barnes also thanked Officer Garcia, the police department, and the fire department. He stated it took many people to make the City run efficiently. View minutes, page 2

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — January 23, 2018:

  • Council Member Barnes asked if they could apply for the grant every three years. Chief Falslev said the grant was offered every year; they would apply every year but the process was highly competitive. Council Member Barnes said he was grateful for the police department and remarked that this was a benefit to the community. View minutes, page 6 · View minutes, page 7

  • Council Member Barnes asked if they could clarify the $12,000 overtime for the police department in Section 3.4. He also noted that in Section 4.3 there was a typo that needed to be fixed. View minutes, page 9
