CITY OFFICIAL RECORDS

Wright, Kasey

Overview

Kasey Wright previously served as legal counsel to American Fork City. This page collects council records documenting that role.

Employment

January 23, 2018 council minutes list Wright as legal counsel and record his advice concerning the city’s agreement with the Chamber of Commerce. These minutes establish his role at that meeting, rather than a complete employment timeline.
