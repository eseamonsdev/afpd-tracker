City Official Remarks

The remarks below are presented as recorded in American Fork City Council meeting minutes.

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — August 25, 2026:

  • Council Member Hunter said the proposal was consistent with comments residents made during the 2021 Truth in Taxation process favoring smaller, incremental adjustments instead of infrequent, substantial increases. He cautioned against treating annual increases as automatic and emphasized that each adjustment should still receive careful scrutiny and consideration of the burden placed on taxpayers. View minutes, pages 5–6

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 28, 2026:

  • Council Member Hunter thanked Mr. Bunker, the Chamber of Commerce, City staff, the Parks Department, law enforcement, and everyone involved in Steel Days, noting the extensive planning and countless behind-the-scenes tasks required to make the celebration successful. He specifically thanked the Police Department for managing traffic at the boat harbor on July 24. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 23, 2026:

  • Council Member Hunter emphasized that the interim budget included the firefighter positions and that the Council would determine final funding priorities during the Truth in Taxation process. He later stated that he believed there was sufficient Council support to fund the firefighter positions regardless of the final property-tax decision and that the budget could be amended if necessary. View minutes, pages 8 and 10

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — June 9, 2026:

  • Council Member Hunter thanked everyone who attended the meeting and participated in public comment, expressing appreciation for the community's engagement and the passion shown. Council Member Hunter also expressed appreciation for the American Fork Police Department. He shared that his family had recently experienced a situation in which they were the target of an incident and publicly thanked the officers who responded for their assistance. View minutes, page 5

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — August 26, 2025:

  • Council Member Hunter questioned whether moving the two workers to another state for employment resembled trafficking, referring to material submitted by the business concerning forced or suspected human trafficking. He also asked whether investigators had collected records that could connect cash payments to patrons' visits. View minutes, page 8

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — July 26, 2022:

  • Council Member Hunter stated that it might benefit the City to retain property designated for public facilities and that the Council needed to consider whether additional space was needed for the Police Department. View minutes, page 3

  • American Fork City Council — Regular Session — September 28, 2021:

  • Council Member Hunter praised the Fire and Police Departments for their invaluable service to the community and pledged support to those groups. View minutes, page 3
