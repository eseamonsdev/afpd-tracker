CIVILIAN EMPLOYEE RECORDS

Frost, Kenneth R.

Overview

This page brings together available employment and compensation records related to Kenneth R. Frost.

Kenneth R. Frost is listed as a crossing guard in the latest available Transparent Utah records for fiscal year 2026.

He is the father of American Fork Mayor Bradley J. Frost.

Employment

Transparent Utah records list Kenneth R. Frost's work for American Fork from fiscal year 2021 through 2026. The latest listed position is Crossing Guard.

A separate American Fork entry in 2020 is labeled Senior Citizens. The crossing guard entries begin in 2021.

These years are based on reported pay, not exact hire or departure dates. Records through 2026 suggest continued employment, but do not confirm current status.

In February 2020, Mayor Brad Frost recommended Kenneth Frost for appointment to the Senior Center Board. At the February 11 council meeting, the mayor disclosed that Kenneth was his father. The council approved the appointment unanimously, with all five council members voting in favor. This was a board appointment, separate from his crossing guard employment.
