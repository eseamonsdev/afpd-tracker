Police Radio Resources

References for understanding agency call signs and radio IDs heard in AFPD recordings.

Agency call signs

Agency call signs identify a jurisdiction or agency in a spoken radio exchange. For example, 5J refers to American Fork, 7J to Pleasant Grove, and 8J to Lehi.

Radio-ID references

A numeric radio ID identifies a transmitting radio. It is distinct from a spoken agency call sign and from a talkgroup, which identifies a radio channel or conversation group.
