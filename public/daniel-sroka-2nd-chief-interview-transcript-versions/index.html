Daniel Sroka’s Second Interview with Chief Paul — Transcript Versions

Daniel Sroka met with Chief Cameron Paul on September 16, 2026. This was his second interview, following the August 10 meeting with Chief Paul and Mayor Bradley Frost.

How the transcript was prepared

The original rough transcript was generated using an automated live speech-to-text app. It was then corrected and revised with AI assistance, followed by manual revisions by Eric Seamons and Daniel Sroka. The versions below preserve that progression, from the original app output to the final participant-reviewed transcript.

No audio recording was available for verification during this editing process. The revised transcript is an edited reconstruction, rather than a verified verbatim record. Earlier drafts retain transcription errors, unresolved passages, and review notes that were addressed in later versions.
