OFFICER RECORDS

Lott, Shawn E.

Overview

Shawn E. Lott was born on 04/20/1988. He is from Riverton, Utah, and graduated from Riverton High School in 2006. He graduated from the Salt Lake Community College Police Academy in April 2014. He has worked for the American Fork Police Department since 2014. He is married to Hollie M. Lott, whose maiden name is Gurr.

Employment

Shawn E. Lott has worked for the American Fork Police Department since 2014, following his graduation from the Salt Lake Community College Police Academy that April. His career has included patrol and detective assignments, and he was listed as a police sergeant in 2025.

His investigative assignments have included the Utah County Major Crimes Task Force and the Special Enforcement Team (SET), identified in a search-warrant affidavit. His task-force work included narcotics investigations. In 2023, he received the Utah Chiefs of Police Association’s Officer of the Year award in the midsize-agency category for his work during 2022.

Alongside his police employment, Lott worked as a noncredit public-safety instructor at Utah Valley University from 2022 through 2025, with additional specialist entries in 2023 and 2024. He also serves on the board of the Utah Gang Investigators Association.
