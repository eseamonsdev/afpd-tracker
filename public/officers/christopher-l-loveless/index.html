OFFICER RECORDS

Loveless, Christopher L.

Overview

Christopher L. Loveless worked for the American Fork Police Department from September 3, 2019, through February 25, 2020. He resigned in lieu of termination following an investigation into a pursuit collision and false statements in his crime report and probable cause statements. In June 2021, the Utah POST Council suspended his peace officer certification for two years for falsification of a government record.

POST Discipline

According to the POST disciplinary proceedings, on February 22, 2020, Christopher L. Loveless accelerated his patrol vehicle during a pursuit and crashed into the back of the suspect vehicle. He falsely stated in his crime report and probable cause statements that his brakes had overheated and were not working to their full potential, preventing him from stopping properly.

During the department’s investigation, Loveless admitted accelerating into the suspect vehicle. He made the same admission during a subsequent POST Garrity interview and said he did not recall using his brakes during the pursuit. He resigned from the American Fork Police Department in lieu of termination on February 25, 2020.

On June 22, 2021, the Utah POST Council voted to suspend Loveless’s peace officer certification for two years for falsification of a government record.
