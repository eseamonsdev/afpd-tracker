Connelly, Christian C.

Overview

Christian C. Connelly worked for the American Fork Police Department from April 11, 2019, through November 2, 2022. Later that month, he began working for the Provo City Constable’s Office. In December 2024, the Utah POST Council revoked his peace officer certification following disciplinary proceedings concerning false overtime entries and repeated dishonesty.

POST Discipline

According to the POST disciplinary proceedings, Christian C. Connelly recorded overtime on his timecard for a hearing after the prosecutor’s legal assistant told him he no longer needed to attend. He subsequently lied to the department multiple times about the overtime entries, including during a Garrity interview. The disciplinary allegations were lying under Garrity and falsification of government records.

On December 9, 2024, the POST Council voted to revoke Connelly’s peace officer certification.
