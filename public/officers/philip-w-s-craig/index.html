OFFICER RECORDS

Craig, Philip W. S.

Overview

Officer Philip W. S. Craig worked for the American Fork Police Department from May 2018 through October 2021.

Craig began working for the Utah County Sheriff’s Office in October 2021. This page includes his background and employment information, as well as records concerning his involvement in the 2021 Janene Thorpe encounter.

Background

Officer Philip William Starlin Craig was born on April 8, 1994. He is from American Fork, Utah, and graduated from American Fork High School in 2012. In June 2015, he married Amber Bardin, who now goes by Amber Craig.

Employment

Officer Philip W. S. Craig worked for the American Fork Police Department from May 1, 2018, through October 2, 2021. During that employment, he was involved in the April 23, 2021, Janene Thorpe encounter. The Thorpes later named Craig as a defendant in a federal lawsuit filed in April 2023.

Craig began working for the Utah County Sheriff’s Office in October 2021. Public compensation records from 2022 through 2025 indicate that he continued working there through at least 2025.
