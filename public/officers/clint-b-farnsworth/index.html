Employment

Officer Farnsworth worked for the American Fork Police Department from August 9, 2017, to November 3, 2022.

After leaving American Fork, Farnsworth joined the Utah County Sheriff’s Office. Public payroll records list him as a Deputy Sheriff II in the Enforcement Division from 2022 through 2025.
