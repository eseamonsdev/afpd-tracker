About Radio IDs

A radio ID is the numeric identifier transmitted by a radio device and shown in recording metadata. An officer may use more than one radio, and a device may be shared or reassigned. A radio ID is distinct from the officer’s spoken call sign.

Radio associations use the same dated evidence and exclusions as the radio resources table. Evidence counts describe associations, not proof of identity or transcript verification.

  • March 8, 2026: Tonga interviewed Benjamin Adams, reviewed his camera roll with consent, and photographed relevant text messages. He then spoke with Ben and Tyler Shaw at the vehicle stop and issued trespass notices. His account appears on pages 5–7 of the incident report.   • March 11, 2026: Tonga handcuffed Ben after the interviews, transported him to Utah County Jail, and booked him there. The March 11 probable-cause statement identifies Tonga as the submitting officer. See pages 17 and 30 of the report and page 2 of the statement.
