About Radio IDs

A radio ID is the numeric identifier transmitted by a radio device and shown in recording metadata. An officer may use more than one radio, and a device may be shared or reassigned. A radio ID is distinct from the officer’s spoken call sign.

Radio associations use the same dated evidence and exclusions as the radio resources table. Evidence counts describe associations, not proof of identity or transcript verification.
