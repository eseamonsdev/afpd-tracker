OFFICER RECORDS

Paul, Cameron J.

Overview

Cameron James Paul is the chief of the American Fork Police Department. He has worked for the department since September 2008 and has led it since September 2021, first as interim chief and then as permanent chief.

As of September 2026, Paul has led the department for almost five years, including more than four and a half years as permanent chief. This profile brings together available background, employment, appointment, and public compensation records.

Background

Cameron James Paul was born on February 7, 1985. He married Stacy Barden in December 2009. She publicly uses the name Stacy Barden Paul.

Employment

Cameron James Paul began working for the American Fork Police Department on September 15, 2008. He later became a lieutenant.

Following Chief Darren Falslev’s retirement, effective September 29, 2021, Paul became interim police chief while the city conducted a formal selection process. American Fork announced Paul’s selection as its permanent chief of police on March 17, 2022.

As of September 24, 2026, Paul has served as permanent police chief for approximately four years and six months. Including his interim service, he has led the department for almost five years.
