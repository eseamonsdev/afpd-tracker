About Radio IDs

A radio ID is the numeric identifier transmitted by a radio device and shown in recording metadata. An officer may use more than one radio, and a device may be shared or reassigned. A radio ID is distinct from the officer’s spoken call sign.

Radio associations use the same dated evidence and exclusions as the radio resources table. Evidence counts describe associations, not proof of identity or transcript verification.

Police Encounters

  • Officer Steven G. Hawkins was involved in the following police encounters:

  • Ben Schneider (Reckless Ben)

  • March 9, 2026: Hawkins spoke with Christian Morgan and issued a trespass warning, then stopped the vehicle associated with the visit. He conducted sobriety tests on Tyler Shaw, requested a police-dog sniff, and searched the vehicle with Fraughton. The report records a .000 breath-test result and release from the scene. See pages 6–9.

  • Hawkins wrote the March 9 narrative and acknowledged that his body-camera microphone was muted during part of the investigation. Attorney John Bryan’s review questions the length of the stop and highlights officers’ discussion of frightening the occupants and whether they had charges. These are concerns raised in Bryan’s analysis, rather than court findings.
