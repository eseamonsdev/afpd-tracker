About Radio IDs

A radio ID is the numeric identifier transmitted by a radio device and shown in recording metadata. An officer may use more than one radio, and a device may be shared or reassigned. A radio ID is distinct from the officer’s spoken call sign.

Radio associations use the same dated evidence and exclusions as the radio resources table. Evidence counts describe associations, not proof of identity or transcript verification.

Police Encounters

  • Ben Schneider (Reckless Ben)

  • March 10, 2026: Sumbot investigated the complaint and spoke with Ben about serving civil papers. He contacted the Marion County Circuit Court, which confirmed that the case was valid and filed but had no date set. He then arrested, handcuffed, and searched Ben, and the sign was booked into evidence. See pages 10–12. Sumbot wrote the March 10 investigation narrative; his later request is documented in Nicosia’s May 27 supplement.

  • May 26–27, 2026: On May 26, Sumbot forwarded Nicosia a request from prosecutor Russell Farr to download relevant YouTube and Patreon material. He supplied the account links. See page 15 of the March 10 report.
