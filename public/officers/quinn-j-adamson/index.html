Related Police Encounters

Sergeant Quinn J. Adamson was involved in the April 17, 2020, encounter with Cody Greenland and was named as a defendant in Greenland’s federal civil-rights lawsuit. The complaint alleged that Adamson tased Greenland multiple times without warning while officers had him pinned to the ground. The case was later settled for an unspecified amount and dismissed with prejudice.

Lieutenant Quinn J. Adamson was also involved in the Reckless Ben matter. Available records identify him as the lieutenant positioned on the perimeter during the search and as the officer who interrupted Benjamin Schneider’s recording and took possession of the recording device.
