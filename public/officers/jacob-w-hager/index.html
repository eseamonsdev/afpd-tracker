OFFICER RECORDS

Hager, Jacob W.

Overview

Officer Jacob W. Hager was employed by the American Fork Police Department from September 26, 2016, through June 26, 2024. He resigned before the department completed an internal affairs investigation into his sexual relationship with fellow Officer Hope N. Groesbeck, which included sexual activity while they were on duty.

This profile includes employment data and records from the Utah POST disciplinary proceedings, in which the POST Council suspended his peace officer certification for two years.

POST Discipline

According to the POST disciplinary proceedings, Officer Jacob W. Hager had sexual intercourse with Officer Hope N. Groesbeck while on duty, in department vehicles in public locations. Both officers estimated that this occurred between 40 and 50 times during their sexual relationship, which lasted from October 2023 through June 2024.

Officer Hager was present at the hearing and he gave a statement apologizing to his wife, his family, and the department for all the decisions he made. Chief Paul sent in a letter on behalf of Officer Hager to the POST council.

Hager self-reported the violation to the American Fork Police Department. He resigned on June 26, 2024, before the investigation concluded. Because he was the one who reported the violation to the department, the Post council voted to suspend his peace officer certification for two years instead of the three and a half in the case of Officer Groesbeck.
