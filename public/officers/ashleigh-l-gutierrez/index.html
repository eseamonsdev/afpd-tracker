Employment

Officer Gutierrez began working for the American Fork Police Department on April 30, 2019, as a Communities That Care Coordinator.

She began working as a law enforcement officer for the department on November 2, 2020.

Gutierrez served as a school resource officer (SRO) at American Fork High School. In the school’s January 27, 2023 newsletter, she described 2022–23 as her first year in the role. The school also identified her as an SRO in its November 22, 2024 newsletter.
