Lunt, Austin S.

Overview

This page brings together available employment and compensation records related to Austin S. Lunt.

Employment

Officer Lunt worked for the American Fork Police Department from May 2, 2016 to April 4, 2025.

After leaving the American Fork Police Department, Lunt joined the Spanish Fork Police Department in March 2025. Spanish Fork formally introduced him as one of its newest police officers during a May 2025 City Council meeting.

Before working at the American Fork Police Department, Lunt held law-enforcement positions with Salt Lake County, Sanpete County, Gunnison City, Centerfield City, Ephraim City, and Spring City.
