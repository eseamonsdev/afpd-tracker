Police Encounters

  • Officer Cole G. Richardson was involved in the following police encounters:

  • Ben Schneider (Reckless Ben)

  • March 8, 2026: Richardson stopped the vehicle driven by Tyler Shaw, with Ben as a passenger. His supplement says he observed stop-sign violations, took no action on those traffic offenses, and turned the investigation over to Tonga. See page 8. Richardson wrote supplements for the March 8 and March 11 incidents and the March 11 warrant affidavit.

  • The March 8 dashcam shows the car pause before the turn, around 13:13:32–33, contrary to the report’s claim that it did not stop completely. The required stopping location is a separate question. In a later interview, summarized from a rough app transcript, Chief Paul said Richardson had misremembered the stop-sign statute and that he did not believe a stop-sign violation occurred; Paul maintained that another basis for the stop existed.

  • March 11, 2026: Richardson questioned the group about the sign and fundraiser, checked with neighboring property owners, and told Sheldon he intended to seize his phone. Richardson’s account says he and Adamson believed Sheldon was trying to destroy evidence. He grabbed Sheldon’s right arm, helped restrain and handcuff him, searched him, and transported him to AFPD. See pages 8–9 and 18.

  • March 11, 2026: Richardson requested the warrant and wrote the operation plan. His supplement identifies the entry order and assigns Adamson to the perimeter. During the operation, Richardson covered the doorway with his patrol rifle while Tonga called people outside and placed them in handcuffs. See page 11 and the warrant packet.

  • March 11, 2026: The booking sheet lists Richardson as the arresting officer. The supplements separately describe Tonga’s later handcuffing, jail transport, and booking. These entries record different parts of the encounter and are kept distinct here.
