    • Benjamin Gorman · October 29, 2026

      Daniel Sroka discusses AFPD accountability with Benjamin Gorman

      American Fork resident Daniel Sroka joins Benjamin Gorman to discuss his meetings with Mayor Bradley Frost and Police Chief Cameron Paul following the police encounters involving Reckless Ben and others.

      Their conversation covers earlier AFPD cases, the chief’s responses to questions about the arrests, body-camera muting and redactions, and the role of citizen participation in police oversight.

      Gorman is a former co-owner, with his wife Chrystal Law-Gorman, of the Salem/Keizer, Oregon Bricks & Minifigs store involved in the LEGO consignment dispute that prompted Reckless Ben’s investigation.

    • AFPD Accountability · October 27, 2026

      K-9 records obtained through GRAMA request

      On October 27, 2026, AFPD Accountability obtained American Fork Police Department K-9 records through a Government Records Access and Management Act (GRAMA) records request.

      The records include a list of 306 K-9 support incidents covering January 1, 2025–October 13, 2026, with incident numbers, reported dates and times, area codes, agencies, and dispositions. A separate July 2025–July 2026 spreadsheet lists cases and zones, interest and search indicators, recorded finds, and apprehension-related entries.

      The records also include a likely connection to Reckless Ben: K-9 support case 26AF02009 was reported March 9, 2026, at 7:59:44 p.m. and is listed with “No Find” in the spreadsheet. The connection is not confirmed, but highly likely: the timestamp falls during that incident, and the case number is only two numbers after the associated harassment report, 26AF02007. This suggests the K-9 officer may have submitted a separate incident report for the same encounter.

    • Clearly Established · October 21, 2026

      Chief Walked Back the LEGO Raid — Now the Transcript Is Out

      Clearly Established attorney Brandon Grable reviews Daniel Sroka’s released transcript of his September 16, 2026 meeting with Chief Cameron Paul. The video includes interview excerpts with Sroka, plaintiffs Steven Allen and Sheldon Norcross, and their attorney, Solomon Radner.

      Grable examines the warrant affidavit, handcuffing and transporting the occupants, Sheldon Norcross’s phone seizure, missing and muted body-camera recordings, redactions, and the chief’s promised follow-up. He also addresses the portions of the police response that the chief continued to defend.

      The video distinguishes the citizen-prepared meeting transcript from an audio recording and attributes the chief’s remarks to that transcript.
