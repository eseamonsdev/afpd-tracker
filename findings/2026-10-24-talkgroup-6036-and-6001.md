# Talkgroup 6036 findings and connections to 6001

Analysis date: October 24, 2026. All times are America/Denver (Mountain daylight time).

## Scope and limitations

This analysis compares all 133 preserved talkgroup 6036 recordings, spanning September 25–October 23, with 1,962 talkgroup 6001 entries on the same ten dates. Source snapshot: `0d5903f0541741ba0093354b9da2855d61c2b6ac`.

The 6036 audio totals 468.736 seconds (7 minutes 48.736 seconds). Every recording received independent original-audio and filtered-audio Whisper small.en passes, followed by AI comparison. The filter was `highpass=f=200,lowpass=f=3400,afftdn=nf=-25`. All 133 transcript entries remain unverified; 79 contain bracketed uncertainty. Agreement between ASR passes is not audio verification. Some 6001 entries have separately verified transcripts; those flags are preserved in the source manifests.

Clip numbers below are chronological and local to each date and talkgroup. Findings describe this preserved set, not a guarantee that every transmission was captured. Timing proximity alone does not establish a common incident or speaker identity.

## Main findings

1. 6036 adds substantial late-evening activity on October 23 after the final 6001 recording.
2. October 8 contains investigative coordination involving warrants, phone-related information, and vehicle observation. A shared recorded source radio ID links the channel metadata, although the common incident is not proven.
3. September 25 provides a strong connection between a race announcement on 6001 and event coordination on 6036.
4. October 22 has a close wording and timing match to an existing search on 6001.
5. The sparse remaining dates do not support broad conclusions or confident incident connections.
